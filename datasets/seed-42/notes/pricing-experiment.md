# Pricing experiment — read-out

Ran the simplified three-tier page against the old five-tier page for four weeks.

Trial starts went up 22%. Trial-to-paid was flat. Average contract value fell about 9%, entirely because the middle tier absorbed accounts that used to land on the top one.

Read: the page is better and the tiering is worse. The middle tier is priced too close to the top for anyone to feel the difference.

Nothing here is conclusive at this volume. Four weeks and sixty trials is a direction, not a result.
