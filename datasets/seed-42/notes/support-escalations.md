# Support escalations — monthly look

Nineteen escalations this month, down from twenty-six. Two thirds of them were the same three questions: export formats, connector permissions, and what the sync window actually guarantees.

All three are documentation problems wearing support tickets as a disguise.

One escalation was a genuine bug in the audit export and it was fixed inside a day.

No account escalated twice. That is the number worth watching, and it is the one that looked bad in the spring.
